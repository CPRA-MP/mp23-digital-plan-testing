## Understanding a Future Without Action

As part of the MP29 analysis, CPRA uses models to evaluate potential changes across a range of coastal indicators, including land area, water depth, habitat, wetland condition, flood risk, and regional patterns.

FWOA projections help clarify the scale, timing, and location of potential coastal change using updated landscape information and assumptions developed for MP29. Land and habitat results come from landscape modeling, while flood-depth and damage results come from storm surge, wave, and risk modeling.
