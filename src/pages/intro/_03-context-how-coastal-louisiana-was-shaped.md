## Context: How Coastal Louisiana Was Shaped

FWOA projections are easier to interpret when placed within the longer coastal story. To understand them, it is important to understand the forces that have shaped coastal Louisiana over time. The coast reflects three broad kinds of influence: long-term environmental change, periodic extreme events, and human decisions about how land, water, infrastructure, restoration, and risk are managed.

Some changes occur gradually over decades. Others happen quickly during storms, floods, droughts, construction, or restoration work.
