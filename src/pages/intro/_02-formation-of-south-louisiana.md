## Formation of South Louisiana

Coastal Louisiana’s current landscape is the result of both natural processes and human decisions over time. Sediment, storms, subsidence, flooding, and habitat change have built and reshaped the coast. That change has influenced how water moves through the landscape and how wetlands and other habitats function.

Communities, industries, navigation, and flood protection expanded, with infrastructure built to both support the working coast and reduce risk. Today, restoration efforts, watershed planning, and coastal management have become central to Louisiana’s ongoing response to change.
