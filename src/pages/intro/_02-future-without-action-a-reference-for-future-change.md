## Future Without Action: A Reference for Future Change

Future Without Action (FWOA) modeling evaluates how coastal Louisiana may change over time without additional restoration or risk-reduction projects beyond those already included in the model. The projections provide a reference point for understanding future needs, comparing potential outcomes, and supporting informed conversations about restoration and risk reduction.

For MP29, FWOA projections use the updated assumptions, landscape information, and future scenarios developed for the MP29 analysis. The results help clarify the scale, timing, and location of potential future risks and support comparison with possible restoration and risk-reduction approaches.

Because future conditions remain uncertain and outcomes will vary across the region, the FWOA condition should be understood as a planning scenario rather than a prediction of one fixed future.
