## Living with Water

People have lived in coastal Louisiana for thousands of years, adapting to a landscape defined by changing land and water. Indigenous communities and those who later settled here adapted to life in this dynamic environment shaped by shoreline erosion, seasonal flooding, subsidence, hurricanes, and storm surge.

Traditional adaptation and flood mitigation practices included strategic settlements, seasonal relocation, elevated storage and structures, ecological stewardship, and reinforcing natural river levees with sediment.
