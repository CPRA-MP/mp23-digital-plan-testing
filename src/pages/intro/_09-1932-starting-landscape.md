## 1932 Starting Landscape

The 1930s mark the beginning of a more consistent coastwide record of land and water change. Aerial imagery made it possible to see coastal patterns more clearly and compare later mapped conditions to an early coastwide snapshot.

The coast shown in this early record had already been shaped by long-running environmental processes and human decisions. Large connected wetland systems remained visible, while flood-control projects, spillways, and navigation improvements were already part of the coastal landscape.
