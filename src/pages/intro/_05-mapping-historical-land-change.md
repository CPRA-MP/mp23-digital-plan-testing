## Mapping Historical Land Change

It is also possible to determine the presence or absence of land and water within the landscape and produce a map from that information. From aerial photography in 1932 to satellite imagery today, technological advancements have improved our understanding of the coast, as well as how we measure and document change.

Take a moment to view this map of observed historical land change over the past century.

As you scroll, pay attention to the changes that are visible across the coast over time. Where does land become water? Where does new land appear? How did the coast of 100 years ago change to become the coast of today?
