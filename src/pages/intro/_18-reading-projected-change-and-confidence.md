## Reading Projected Change and Confidence

To explore future change, the Louisiana Coastal Master Plan uses predictive models to simulate a variety of future environmental conditions. To account for uncertainty within plan development, the model is run many times with different assumptions to show a range of possible outcomes. Areas where results are consistent across runs show higher confidence, while areas with more variation indicate where the future landscape is less certain.

Considering projections in the context of confidence in model outputs is important for imagining future coastal challenges and developing project concepts to address those challenges.
