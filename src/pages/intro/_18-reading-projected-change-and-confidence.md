## Reading Projected Change and Confidence

To assess future change, the MP29 analysis uses models to explore how coastal conditions may change under different environmental scenarios. Land and habitat results come from ICM-based modeling, while flood-depth and damage results come from storm surge, wave, and risk modeling.

When results are similar across scenarios, there is more confidence that pattern of change will be realized. Areas with more variation show where future conditions are less certain.

Sea level rise curves and cumulative land area change curves will continue to be shown alongside the projection maps so users can read map patterns with the scenario conditions and overall land-change trajectory in view.
